QA Heatwave

QA Heatwave is an 8mm carpet underlay designed for use with under floor heating. It is perfect for domestic installations and will make your carpets feel softer. With a TOG rating of 1.2, it will maximize heat. It also offers 36dB sound reduction, making your carpet and home quieter.
