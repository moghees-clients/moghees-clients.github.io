New Kingston

Introducing New Kingston, a versatile carpet that brings comfort and style to any home. Available in a range of contemporary colors, this carpet features a luxurious 11mm pile height, providing a soft and inviting feel underfoot. With its durable heavy felt backing, New Kingston is designed to withstand everyday domestic use, making it an ideal choice for any room. Choose from 4m and 5m widths to fit your space perfectly and enjoy a blend of quality and elegance that enhances your interior.
