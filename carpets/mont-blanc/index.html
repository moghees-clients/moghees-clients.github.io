Mont Blanc

Introducing Mont Blanc, a carpet that embodies both luxury and durability. Crafted with 100% Polypropylene pile content, Mont Blanc offers unmatched resilience and comfort. Available in widths of 4m and 5m, it seamlessly fits various room dimensions. Its sturdy Action Backing ensures stability, making it perfect for heavy domestic use. Additionally, Mont Blanc is bleach-cleanable, allowing for effortless maintenance and long-lasting beauty. Elevate your home with Mont Blanc, where style meets practicality.
