Treasure Island

Discover the allure of Treasure Island carpet, designed to bring charm and durability to your home. Crafted from 100% Polypropylene, this carpet ensures both resilience and comfort. Available in a versatile 4m width, Treasure Island effortlessly fits various room sizes. Its felt backing provides a soft underfoot feel while maintaining stability. Perfect for general domestic use, Treasure Island carpet is also bleach-cleanable, offering easy maintenance and lasting elegance. Transform your space into a treasure trove of comfort and style with Treasure Island carpet.
