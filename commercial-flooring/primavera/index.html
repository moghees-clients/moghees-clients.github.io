Primavera

Get ready to experience sophistication with Primavera, the ultimate choice for upscale commercial carpeting. Crafted from 100% Luxury Saxony Polypropylene, Primavera offers a luxurious feel underfoot, elevating any space with its plush texture and refined aesthetic. Available in a generous 4m width and with roll lengths of up to 30m, Primavera ensures seamless coverage for large-scale projects. Its Rot Proof Water Resistant Gel backing provides durability and resilience, perfect for heavy contact areas. Tested under the Hot Metal Nut Test BS 4790, Primavera boasts a low radius of ignition, offering peace of mind in terms of flammability concerns. Transform your commercial environment with Primavera – where elegance meets durability.
