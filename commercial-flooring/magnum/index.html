Magnum

The ultimate choice for commercial flooring solutions. Crafted from 100% Polypropylene, Magnum offers unbeatable durability and resilience to heavy contact, making it ideal for high-traffic areas. Available in a convenient 2m width and with roll lengths of up to 25m, Magnum ensures seamless installation in any space. Its Rot Proof Water Resistant Gel backing guarantees longevity, while its low radius of ignition, tested under the Hot Metal Nut Test BS 4790, ensures peace of mind in terms of flammability concerns. Elevate your commercial space with Magnum – where performance meets reliability.
